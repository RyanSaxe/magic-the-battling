## Overview

The fight phase is where you play a quick mini-game of magic with a few modified rules.

## Magic Rule Modifications

- Each player starts with 10 life
- Libraries start empty, and you cannot lose the game due to not being able to draw cards.
- Conceding is not allowed because [Treasure tokens](doc:game-pieces#treasures) created during a fight persist afterwards.
- A draw is considered a loss for both players.

## Before the Fight Starts

- All basic lands selected during the [build phase](doc:build) start on the battlefield untapped.
- All [treasures](doc:game-pieces#treasures) a player has at the beginning of the fight phase will start on their battlefield.
- All [poison counters](doc:game-pieces#poison) a player has is attached to them during the fight.
- The player with the most [poison counters](doc:game-pieces#poison) gets their choice of play/draw. If players are tied, the choosing player is selected randomly. In the finals, after the first game, the loser of the previous game chooses instead.
- Each player's starting hand and sideboard become exactly what was selected during the [build phase](doc:build).

## After the Fight Ends

- The losing player(s) get N [poison counters](doc:game-pieces#poison), where N is 1 + the number of revealed [upgrades](doc:game-pieces#upgrades) their opponent has applied to cards they submitted for the fight: their submitted hand, plus their selected companion if they have one. An upgrade counts only once it has been revealed, in this fight or an earlier one. If you are not playing with upgrades, N is Fibonacci(stage - 2).
- Each player keeps up to 5 [treasures](doc:game-pieces#treasures) that are still on their battlefield and exiles the rest.

## Controls

:::pointer
- You can drag and drop cards between any zones, including your opponent's zones in order to make casting cards like Reanimate easier to resolve.
- Click a card, then click empty space in another zone to move it there.
- Click "actions" on the bottom left to see what actions you can take.
  - If a card is selected, some extra actions will appear.
  - Right clicking on a card will also bring up the action options for that card.
- Click "submit" on the bottom right when you know the result of the game to move onto the next phase.
:::

:::touch
- Press and hold a card, then drag it to any zone, including your opponent's zones, to make cards like Reanimate easier to resolve.
- Tap a card, then tap empty space in another zone to move it there.
- Double-tap a card to tap or untap it.
- Tap “Actions” at the bottom left to see what actions you can take.
  - Tap a card first to add its own actions: Tap/Untap, Flip, Move To and more.
- Tap “Submit Result” at the bottom right when you know the result of the game to move on to the next phase.
:::
